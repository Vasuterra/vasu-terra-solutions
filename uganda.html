GIS, Remote Sensing & Water Resource Analytics for Uganda

Vasu Terra Solutions (VTS) offers remote GIS, satellite-based analysis, hydrology and environmental mapping support for project teams considering work in Uganda. We support evidence-based decisions with reproducible geospatial workflows, thematic mapping and project-specific interpretation.

Applications relevant to Uganda

Our consultancy can support watershed delineation, groundwater potential mapping, agricultural monitoring and land-use classification, particularly where geospatial information can inform catchment management and resource planning.

Services

  • Groundwater potential zoning
  • LULC mapping and change detection
  • Drainage density analysis
  • Flood-risk mapping

How we work

We define a project area and deliverables, review available satellite and ancillary datasets, prepare spatial analyses, and document methods and limitations. Field validation and local regulatory advice require local partners or suitably qualified professionals where applicable.

Work with VTS

Discuss a location, study boundary, data requirements and technical deliverables. Support is offered subject to project scope, data availability and any applicable local requirements.
