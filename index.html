GIS, Remote Sensing & Environmental Consulting Services in India

Agriculture still supports a majority of livelihoods across India, and what it produces depends on resources that are hard to assess from the ground alone — how much groundwater is really available, what condition the soil is in, and how water moves across a watershed over a season. Remote Sensing and GIS make it possible to study these at scale: mapping groundwater potential and recharge zones, selecting sites for irrigation infrastructure, check dams and rainwater harvesting structures, and assessing soil erosion and conservation needs across a catchment. This is the intersection where a good part of our work sits — agricultural relevance, backed by geospatial method.
