About the Firm

Vasu Terra Solutions is a geospatial consulting firm specializing in GIS, Remote Sensing, Soil & Water Conservation, Hydrogeology, Environmental Planning, Watershed Management, and AI-driven Spatial Analytics.

Our mission is to transform spatial data into actionable insights that support sustainable development, environmental conservation, infrastructure planning, and water resource management.

We're working to build our footing across government bodies, engineering firms, research institutions, industry, renewable energy companies, and NGOs — one project at a time, combining scientific rigour with modern geospatial technology.

How We Work

Our Process

A fixed sequence, refined across projects, that takes a brief from first call to field-ready recommendation.

01

Project Consultation

We scope the technical problem, study area, deliverables, and success criteria with your team.

02

Data Collection

Satellite imagery, field survey data, DEMs, and secondary records are compiled and quality-checked.

03

GIS & Remote Sensing Analysis

Layers are processed, classified, and cross-validated against ground truth.

04

Spatial Modelling

Hydrological, statistical, or machine-learning models are built and calibrated for the study area.

05

Technical Report Preparation

Findings are documented to the standard your reviewers and regulators expect.

06

Decision Support & Recommendations

We hand off maps, dashboards, and recommendations ready to act on.
